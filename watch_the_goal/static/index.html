    Suggestions

      Match recap

      Each recording has its own scoreboard. Leave fields blank to omit them.

      Captions

      A line on each kept clip slides in along the bottom; clips fade through black between them. Leave a line blank for none.

      Mark the goals

      Drag a box around each goal mouth (up to two). Movement inside these boxes counts as evidence. Optional, and only useful for fixed, wide footage.

      Analysis

      Both are optional. Without them, suggestions come from crowd noise, motion and marked goal areas. Everything runs on this computer.

      Player detector

      Counts players near a marked goal and players bunched together, and ignores goal-box motion caused by someone right in front of the camera. Adds about 1–2 s per minute of footage. Re-analyse a recording to use it.

      AI review

      After analysis, shows the strongest suggestions to a vision model running in Ollama on this computer. It labels each one (likely goal, save, near miss…), checks whether players celebrate right after, reorders them and proposes a trim. About a minute per suggestion. It never changes clips you have kept, skipped or edited.
